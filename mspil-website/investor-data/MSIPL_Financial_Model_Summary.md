# Mahakaushal Sugars & Power Industries Limited (MSIPL)
## Financial Model Summary

### Executive Overview
MSIPL operates an integrated sugar complex with multiple revenue streams from sugar production, ethanol manufacturing, DDGS (Distillers Dried Grains with Solubles), and power generation. The company demonstrates strong growth potential with revenue expected to grow from ₹811 crores in FY26 to ₹1,565 crores in FY30, representing a CAGR of 39.1%.

### Production Capacity
- **Sugar Plant**: 8,00,000 TPA sugarcane crushing capacity
- **Ethanol Plant**: 410 KLPD (123 million liters per annum)
- **Power Generation**: 9.66 MW total (4.66 MW for sale after captive consumption)
- **Operational Days**: 300 days per annum

### Key Financial Ratios

#### Return Ratios
- **Return on Equity (ROE)**: Improving from 27.0% in FY26 to peak at 46.0% in FY28
- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)**: Rising from 15.5% in FY26 to 49.0% in FY28

#### Leverage Ratios
- **Debt/Equity Ratio**: Declining from 2.53x in FY26 to 0.22x in FY30
- **Interest Coverage Ratio**: Improving from 5.90x in FY26 to 59.14x in FY30

#### Efficiency Ratios
- **Inventory Turnover**: 2.75x - 6.56x
- **Receivable Turnover**: 12.92x - 21.28x
- **Fixed Asset Turnover**: Improving from 1.02x in FY26 to 10.05x in FY30

### Working Capital Management
- **Receivable Days**: 
  - Ethanol: 26 days
  - DDGS: 3-7 days
  - Sugar: 1-15 days
  - Power: 15-30 days

### Key Operational Assumptions
1. **Sugar Recovery Rate**: 11% (vs industry average of 10% in UP/Maharashtra)
2. **Ethanol Yield**: 406.96 liters per ton of corn
3. **DDGS Yield**: 280 kg per ton of corn
4. **Corn Starch Content**: 60.72%
5. **Fermentation Efficiency**: 94%

### Pricing Assumptions
- **Ethanol**: ₹71.86 per liter
- **DDGS**: ₹14.50 per kg
- **Sugar**: ₹35 per kg (₹3,500 per quintal)
- **Molasses**: ₹12 per kg
- **Power**: ₹4.50 per unit
- **Sugarcane Procurement**: ₹350 per quintal

### Competitive Advantages
1. **Higher Sugar Recovery**: 11% recovery rate vs 10% industry average provides 10% higher revenue without additional raw material cost
2. **Integrated Operations**: Multiple revenue streams from single raw material base
3. **Location Advantage**: Operations in Madhya Pradesh with favorable sugarcane growing conditions
4. **Operational Efficiency**: Higher recovery rates translate directly to improved margins

### Investment Highlights
1. **Strong Revenue Growth**: 39.1% CAGR over FY25-30
2. **Margin Expansion**: EBITDA margins expanding from 21.2% to 27.8%
3. **Deleveraging**: Debt/Equity reducing from 2.53x to 0.22x
4. **High Returns**: ROE peaking at 46% and ROCE at 49%
5. **Cash Generation**: Strong operational cash flows supporting growth

### Risk Factors
1. Commodity price volatility (sugar, ethanol)
2. Regulatory changes in ethanol blending policies
3. Seasonal dependency on sugarcane availability
4. Weather-related agricultural risks

### Conclusion
MSIPL presents a compelling investment opportunity with strong growth trajectory, improving margins, and excellent return ratios. The company's integrated business model, operational efficiency, and strategic location provide sustainable competitive advantages in the sugar and ethanol industry.
