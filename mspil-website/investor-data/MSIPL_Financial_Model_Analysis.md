# MSIPL Financial Model Analysis & Documentation

## Executive Summary
Mahakaushal Sugars & Power Industries Limited (MSIPL) is poised for exceptional growth with revenue expected to reach ₹1,565 Cr by FY30 from ₹811 Cr in FY26, representing a 39.1% CAGR. The company's integrated business model across sugar, ethanol, DDGS, and power generation provides multiple revenue streams and operational synergies.

## Business Segments Overview

### 1. Sugar Manufacturing
- **Capacity**: 8,00,000 TPA sugarcane crushing
- **Recovery Rate**: 11% (vs 10% industry average)
- **Revenue FY30**: ₹408.02 Cr (26.1% of total)
- **Key Advantage**: 10% higher revenue with same input cost due to superior recovery

### 2. Ethanol Production
- **Capacity**: 410 KLPD (123 million liters/annum)
- **Raw Material**: Corn/Maize
- **Yield**: 406.96 liters/ton
- **Revenue FY30**: ₹924.61 Cr (59.1% of total)
- **Price**: ₹71.86/liter (government-regulated)

### 3. DDGS (Distillers Dried Grains)
- **Production**: 280 kg/ton of corn processed
- **Revenue FY30**: ₹177.94 Cr (11.4% of total)
- **Price**: ₹14.50/kg
- **Market**: Animal feed industry

### 4. Power Generation
- **Capacity**: 9.66 MW total (4.66 MW for sale)
- **Revenue FY30**: ₹54.34 Cr (3.5% of total)
- **Price**: ₹4.50/unit
- **Tax Benefit**: Income tax-free

## Key Assumptions & Sensitivities

2. **Sugar Recovery**: 11% consistent

### Pricing Assumptions

2. **Sugar**: Market-linked with MSP support
3. **DDGS**: Linked to soybean meal prices

2. **Price Volatility**: Sugar and DDGS market prices

4. **Weather Dependency**: Agricultural input risks
