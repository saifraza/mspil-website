MAHAKAUSHAL SUGAR & POWER INDUSTRIES LIMITED
ANNUAL REPORT 2022-23

CHAIRMAN'S MESSAGE

Dear Shareholders,

I am pleased to present the Annual Report of Mahakaushal Sugar & Power Industries Limited for the financial year 2022-23. This year marked a significant phase of strategic consolidation and operational excellence for our company.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS FY 2022-23:

• Total Revenue: ₹285 Crores (Growth of 18% YoY)
• EBITDA: ₹67 Crores (Margin: 23.5%)
• Sugar Production: 95,000 MT
• Power Generation: 48.5 Million Units
• Enhanced Operational Efficiency: 10.8% Sugar Recovery Rate
