MAIN TITLE: "Our Core Business Verticals"
SUBTITLE: "Delve into our synergistic operations, delivering value and promoting sustainability across diverse agri-industrial sectors."

BUSINESS SEGMENTS:
------------------

1. SUGAR MANUFACTURING
Name: "Sugar Manufacturing"
Details: "Our state-of-the-art, automated milling processes boast a capacity of 8,000 TCD. We produce high-quality refined sugar catering to diverse industrial clients and retail consumers, ensuring purity and consistency."
Capacity Data: "8,000 TCD Capacity"

Manufacturing Process:
- Step 1: Sugarcane Reception & Preparation (Cleaning, Shredding)
- Step 2: Juice Extraction (Milling or Diffusion)
- Step 3: Juice Clarification (Heating, Liming, Sulphitation)
- Step 4: Evaporation & Crystallization (Syrup to Massecuite)
- Step 5: Centrifugation & Drying (Crystal Separation & Drying)
- Step 6: Packaging & Dispatch of Sugar
- Step 7: Key Byproducts Utilized
- Byproduct: Bagasse (to Power Plant), Molasses (to Distillery)

2. BIOFUEL (ETHANOL) PRODUCTION
Name: "Biofuel (Ethanol) Production"  
Details: "Leading biofuel production with an annual output of 10 Crore Liters, expanding from 350 KLPD towards 800 KLPD. Our fuel-grade ethanol meets stringent quality standards, contributing significantly to cleaner energy solutions."
Capacity Data: "10 Cr. Ltrs/Annum | 350 → 800 KLPD"

Manufacturing Process:
- Step 1: Molasses/Grain Reception & Preparation
- Step 2: Fermentation (Yeast converts sugars to ethanol)
- Step 3: Distillation & Rectification (Ethanol Separation)
- Step 4: Dehydration (Anhydrous Ethanol for Fuel Blending)
- Step 5: Storage & Dispatch of Ethanol
- Step 6: Byproduct Management
- Byproduct: Spent Wash (to Bio-composting), DDGS (if grain-based, to Animal Feed)

3. CO-GENERATION POWER
Name: "Co-generation Power"
Details: "Our 24 MW cogeneration power plant exemplifies renewable energy commitment. We utilize bagasse for sustainable electricity, exporting 14 MW to the grid, powering communities and industries alike."
Capacity Data: "24 MW Plant | 14 MW Export"

Manufacturing Process:
- Step 1: Bagasse (Fuel) Reception from Sugar Mill
- Step 2: Boiler Firing & High-Pressure Steam Generation
- Step 3: Steam Turbine & Generator Operation for Electricity
- Step 4: Power Distribution (Internal Use & Grid Export)
- Step 5: Ash Management
- Byproduct: Ash (for manure component / brick making)

4. ANIMAL NUTRITION (DDGS)
Name: "Animal Nutrition (DDGS)"
Details: "Producing 200 TPD of premium Distillers Dried Grains with Solubles (DDGS). Our Narsingh Gold & Silver brands offer highly nutritious and protein-rich feed for livestock and poultry sectors."
Capacity Data: "200 TPD DDGS Production"

Manufacturing Process:
- Step 1: Separation of Wet Grains (Post-distillation)
- Step 2: Evaporation & Concentration of Solubles (Thin Stillage)
- Step 3: Drying of Grains with Solubles & Cooling
- Step 4: Packaging & Dispatch of DDGS

5. ORGANIC MANURE PRODUCTION
Manufacturing Process:
- Step 1: Reception of Press Mud (Sugar) & Spent Wash (Distillery)
- Step 2: Windrow Composting & Periodic Aeration
- Step 3: Microbial Decomposition & Maturation of Compost
- Step 4: Screening, Fortification (Optional) & Packaging
